06 / BETTER TASTE

Aroma. Balance.
Your own experience.

Begin with coffee. Ask a clear question, keep the recipe consistent and compare without labels.

A COMPARISON, NOT A PROMISE

Make the tasting meaningful.

01

Match the samples

Use the same coffee batch, dose, brewing equipment, recipe and serving temperature. Change only the application you want to evaluate.

02

Hide the labels

Have someone code the cups A and B. Taste in a balanced order and repeat the comparison rather than judging from one cup.

03

Record what you notice

Describe aroma, acidity, bitterness, body, aftertaste and overall preference. Include neutral results and differences between tasters.

BEYOND THE FIRST CUP

Different products.
Different questions.

Coffee & tea

Explore aroma, balance, texture and finish. Sensory preference is individual; a trial is not evidence of a health benefit.

Wine & whisky

For adults of legal drinking age only. Discuss controlled aroma and flavor comparisons for the specific beverage. Treatment does not establish that alcohol is safer or healthier.

Sauces & cooking

Test the same recipe with matched ingredients. Ask whether a difference remains after preparation and whether more than one taster notices it.
