Conceptual illustration of the application journey.

Large vessels. Long operating hours.

Tankers, cargo ships and cruise vessels use fuel for propulsion and onboard power. These are important application areas in Neipclova’s Fuel Bar experience.

Make every unit of fuel count.

Our team reports substantial fuel-saving observations in marine applications. We assess each case against its route, speed, load, sea conditions and delivered work before stating an application-specific result.

Fuel Bar is part of our experience-led approach to making better use of gasoline and diesel energy.

Neipclova’s team reports observations of improved energy efficiency in Fuel Bar applications. These observations guide further discussion and evaluation; they are not a promise that every vehicle, engine or generator will achieve the same result.

Useful energy

Compare fuel input with distance travelled or electrical output, rather than fuel consumption in isolation.

What needs to be confirmed?

Product specification, suitability, installation method and test evidence should be reviewed for the intended equipment. This page does not specify an unverified operating mechanism or a fixed savings rate.
