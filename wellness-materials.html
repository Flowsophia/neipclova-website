Specialty Yarns & Lasting Comfort

Korean-inspired craft, surface treatments and clothing worth keeping.

Comfort begins in the yarn, continues through the fabric and is experienced at the surface of the garment.

Specialty yarns. Clearly understood materials.

Neipclova explores specially processed yarns and textiles, including polymer-based fibres and natural-derived materials. A natural-looking or soft surface does not change a fibre’s actual composition; product information should identify what it is made from.

Material processing, weave, knit structure and surface finishing shape the garment experience. Our direction brings these choices together with Korean-inspired craft and individual use.

Consider the surface, consider the wearer.

The body encounters fabric through touch, heat, moisture and movement. Discuss the intended texture, breathability, wash behaviour and skin-contact suitability of a particular garment.

Special processing is a design and development area. Any claimed biological or health effect needs evidence specific to the material and intended use.

Antibacterial textiles: define the property.

Where antibacterial performance is proposed, it should be described using the actual textile, test method, organisms tested, result and performance after relevant washing.

Restricting bacterial growth on a fabric is different from preventing infection in the person wearing it. Claims for a garment must match the supporting product-specific evidence.

Material evidence

Composition, treatment identity where disclosed, skin-contact suitability and relevant test documentation.

Use & laundering

Care instructions, treatment durability and the conditions under which performance was evaluated.

Wearer & setting

Infant, maternity and clinical applications need their own suitability review and appropriate professional input.

Design for a longer relationship.

Fit, durability, maintenance and repair help a garment remain useful. Neipclova’s aim is clothing worth keeping, with environmental decisions informed by the whole material and use cycle.
