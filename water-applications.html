Water participates in preparation, dilution and beverage extraction. Neipclova explores reported sensory differences using comparable ingredients, preparation conditions, serving temperature and tasting methods.

Korean ceramic cups: a material encounter.

A ceramic cup connects the drink with its vessel. Neipclova's treated ceramic approach and reported experiences of taste are part of this discussion. A clearer comparison uses the same beverage and serving conditions, with the selected cup as the defined variable.

A pleasant taste does not itself establish food-contact suitability or a health benefit. Confirm suitability and care information for the actual cup and any treatment.
