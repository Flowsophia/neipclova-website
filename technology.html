THE NEIPCLOVA APPROACH

A shared foundation.
A purpose for every application.

Rooted in Pactive Korea’s materials experience and connected with ePactive, Neipclova explores special treatments, surfaces and interfaces across seven areas of everyday life. The application guides the method, the design and the questions we ask.

FROM AN IDEA TO A USEFUL APPLICATION

Understand. Adapt. Evaluate.

01 / DEFINE

Start with the real need.

Identify the material, the user, the operating environment and the improvement being sought.

02 / DEVELOP

Consider the interface.

Explore treatment, finishing and system integration in relation to the material’s intended function.

03 / EVALUATE

Make the comparison clear.

Distinguish a future concept, a reported experience and a demonstrated result under defined conditions.

Our fields share a development philosophy, not a single universal treatment specification. Confidential know-how is discussed within the scope of each project.

01Water

Improve the water. Understand the source.

Filtration is the starting point. Neipclova explores treatment within a water system, with the aim of improving the water available to us rather than promising perfect water. Source quality, intended use and the selected filter remain essential.

APPLICATION PATH

Source water → filtration → treatment approach → application-specific evaluation

Technology in focus

  • Filter selection and system conditions
  • Water quality before and after treatment
  • Maintenance and the intended drinking or process-water use
Explore Water ↗
02Energy

Follow energy from its source to useful work.

Fuel Bar connects our energy story from petroleum plants and fuel handling to engines, generators, vehicles and marine applications. Field observations motivate further evaluation of fuel consumption and operating efficiency. Solar generation and battery storage extend that direction toward a sustainable future.

APPLICATION PATH

Fuel source → handling → engine or generator → useful energy

Technology in focus

  • Fuel Bar and operating-condition comparisons
  • Lite, Drive and King vehicle model discussions
  • Solar integration; Li-ion, Li-polymer and solid-state battery directions

03Smart Farm

Connect the conditions that let life grow.

Good water, suitable soil and a well-managed environment form the base. Our direction connects solar and geothermal energy, energy-management devices, sensing, robotics and AI with biological knowledge. Functional films and packaging carry the conversation beyond cultivation into distribution.

APPLICATION PATH

Growing conditions → sensing → control → harvest → packaging

Technology in focus

  • Water, environmental control and energy use
  • Robotics, optics, semiconductor and sensor systems
  • Beneficial biology, antifog films and produce packaging
Explore Smart Farm ↗
Automation & sensing ↗Packaging ↗Future frontiers ↗
04Wellness

Begin with what the body wears.

Specialty yarns, fabric construction and surface finishing shape how clothing feels in daily life. Neipclova considers comfort, durability and the needs of different wearers, alongside personal accessories. Ring experiences and proposed textile properties are discussed with evidence appropriate to the specific product.

APPLICATION PATH

Yarn → fabric structure → finishing → garment → wearer

Technology in focus

  • Touch, breathability, moisture and care behaviour
  • Treatment durability and product-specific textile evaluation
  • Clothing, personal accessories and Korean-inspired craft
Explore Wellness ↗
Materials & yarns ↗Clothing ↗Ring & accessories ↗
05Korean Traditional Ceramics

Respect the object. Explore the surface.

Korean ceramic craftsmanship gives each piece its character. Neipclova explores a special treatment approach alongside that craft, preserving the importance of the original object, its finish and its intended use. White porcelain and moon jars express a quiet connection between material, maker and everyday space.

APPLICATION PATH

Clay and craft → finished ceramic → treatment discussion → intended use

Technology in focus

  • Original ceramic collections and surface appearance
  • Treatment scope and care requirements
  • Individual product selection and quotation
Explore Korean Traditional Ceramics ↗
06Better Taste

Compare the experience. Care for the ingredient.

Food is culture. Our work considers water, oils, sauces, powders and other ingredients through controlled sensory comparisons, including coffee tasting. Functional food packaging adds a materials perspective to handling and freshness. Taste evaluation and food-contact suitability are separate questions, each requiring its own review.

APPLICATION PATH

Ingredient → defined treatment → sensory comparison → handling and packaging

Technology in focus

  • Coffee, sauces, oils and other ingredient comparisons
  • World food cultures and practical cooking contexts
  • Packaging suitability, freshness and distribution conditions
Explore Better Taste ↗
Ingredients ↗Tasting approach ↗Freshness & packaging ↗
07Eco-friendly Decorative Products

Engineer the surface behind the appearance.

Decorative film, surface coating, lamination and substrate preparation work together as a material system. Homes, architectural spaces, yacht interiors and emerging applications need different specifications. Our approach connects appearance with installation, cleaning, wear and the ability to renew a space.

APPLICATION PATH

Surface finish → decorative layer → bonding interface → substrate

Technology in focus

  • PP design films, tactile finishes and lamination interfaces
  • Residential, architecture and mobility application requirements
  • Moisture, cleaning, durability and adaptable future surfaces
