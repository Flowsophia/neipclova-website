01

Understand the material

Polymer processing, functional films, coating and lamination provide a foundation for asking how a material is made and how its surface or structure may be adapted.

02

Understand the application

Industrial and everyday applications place different demands on a material. We begin with the actual environment, the user and the intended function.

03

Bring disciplines together

Materials, energy, design and practical experience meet in our approach. A good idea becomes more useful when the right people and knowledge are brought together.

04

We distinguish a design concept, a reported experience and a demonstrated result. Treatment is considered within a defined application, with appropriate evidence and review.

04 / THE MEANING WE CARRY

A small symbol.
A generous intention.

Neipclova carries the image of a four-leaf clover: a natural symbol of hope and possibility. We give that symbol a practical purpose—bringing nature and technology together to benefit people and the world around them.

Our work explores special treatments and material approaches across seven fields. We ask whether carefully adapting a material, a surface or an interface can improve the way something performs, feels or serves its purpose.

Each field has its own conditions. The ambition is shared; the method and evidence must fit the application. Our confidential know-how does not replace the need for clear requirements, appropriate evaluation and honest communication.
