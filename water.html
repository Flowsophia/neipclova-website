Better Water.

A well-chosen filter is an important step toward better water for drinking.

OUR PURPOSE

Improve the water you have.

Every water source has its own characteristics. A filter cannot turn every source into perfect water. Our focus is practical improvement: helping the water you already have become better suited to everyday drinking through an appropriate filtration approach.

OUR CORE TECHNOLOGY

The Neipclova approach.

At Neipclova, our core technology is focused on filtration-led water improvement. We place the filter at the heart of the solution, with the aim of improving existing water rather than promising the best possible water in every situation.

THE RIGHT FIT

Start with the source.

A suitable solution begins with understanding the incoming water, selecting an appropriate filter, and maintaining it properly. The level of improvement depends on the source water, the filter used, and operating conditions.

Our commitment is to better water through thoughtful filtration.

Specific performance claims should be supported by testing of the selected filter and source water. Filtration alone does not guarantee that any water source is safe to drink.
