01 / INTERIOR APPLICATIONS

Spaces that work beautifully.

Corporate offices

Workplace surfaces communicate identity while supporting circulation, storage and maintenance.

Positions
Reception desks, meeting-room storage, door faces, partitions and wall panels.
Priorities
Wear at corners, frequent touch, acoustic design coordination and replaceable access panels.
Detail strategy
Locate seams away from high-impact edges where practical. Maintain access to power, data and ventilation.
Project evidence
Approve colour under office lighting and a sample assembly with the chosen substrate and adhesive.

Hospitals & care environments

Public waiting and reception spaces have different requirements from clinical treatment areas. Each zone must be reviewed with the facility team.

Positions
Potential public-area cabinet faces, reception features and non-clinical decorative panels, subject to approval.
Priorities
Cleaning and disinfectant compatibility, accessible joints and edges, and maintainability.
Detail strategy
Review the actual cleaning products and contact times against product instructions. Design details that can be reached and inspected.
Project evidence
The healthcare facility determines suitability. Decorative finishes are not presented as infection-control devices or universally antibacterial solutions.
Discuss this application ↗

Retail interiors

Retail fixtures are renewed more frequently and place brand presentation beside intensive everyday handling.

Positions
Display units, counters, fitting-room fronts, storage and promotional wall panels.
Priorities
Colour consistency, visible finish quality, abrasion, display lighting and practical repair.
Detail strategy
Separate decorative facings from load-bearing fixture design. Coordinate hooks, signs and service access.
Project evidence
Approve a lit mock-up, expected cleaning and impact zones before repeating fixtures.
Discuss this application ↗

Multi-unit refurbishment

Repeated hotel rooms or apartment units need a system: not just an attractive first panel.

Positions
Repeated door fronts, wardrobe panels, guest-room cabinetry and corridor details.
Priorities
Batch traceability, matching finish orientation, unit numbering and controlled installation phases.
Detail strategy
Survey variations between units; agree a reference mock-up, cutting schedule and replacement-stock policy.
Project evidence
Track substrate condition, approved installation records and finish reference by room or unit.

03 / EXTERIOR

The façade is an assembly, not a colour choice.

Exterior architectural panels

Specify the complete system: exposure, panel substrate, bonding construction, edge details and the relevant project requirements.

Exposure
State orientation, climate, UV, humidity and temperature range.
Interfaces
Review panel edges, joints, movement, drainage and corrosion protection.
Approval
Request reports for the actual grade and assembly. Interior film performance cannot be assumed to transfer outdoors.
Procurement
Provide elevation drawings, panel schedule, total area, phased quantities and maintenance expectations.
