Smart Farm Overview

Robotics, AI & Energy

Semiconductors, optics and sensors connecting observation to careful operation.

A growing environment needs both a reliable physical foundation and a way to observe and respond to change.

Water and energy come first.

Neipclova places suitable water, soil or growing media, and the growing environment at the centre of its Smart Farm direction. Energy helps maintain that environment and operate the selected equipment.

Solar & geothermal

Consider solar electricity and site-appropriate geothermal heating or cooling alongside the crop’s needs, climate and available infrastructure.

Generators & batteries

Discuss continuity, backup and storage around the actual electrical and thermal demands of the project.

LDK-family devices

LDK-type energy-support devices belong to application-specific evaluation. Any contribution must be measured in the relevant fuel-powered equipment; no direct enhancement of AI, semiconductor performance or all energy sources is asserted.

Semiconductors, optics and sensor engineering.

Electronic sensing, optical observation and computing bring information into the operating process. Neipclova’s ambition is to connect these disciplines with AI-assisted review and appropriate robotics.

Potential work includes environmental observation, crop imaging, irrigation support, harvesting, sorting and handling. These are development areas, with equipment selection and integration defined for each project.

ILLUSTRATIVE WORKFLOW · NO LIVE SENSOR DATA

Sensors and optics collect observations from the growing environment.

Keep the human purpose in view.

Automation should help operators understand their growing space and perform appropriate tasks. Measurements, calibration, biological expertise and human review remain part of a responsible system.
