Wellness Overview

Clothing for Every Chapter

Underwear, outerwear and the everyday environments we live in.

What we wear follows us through birth, growth, work, care and ageing. Comfort should be considered in the context of that life.

From the first layer to the outer layer.

Underwear, everyday garments, outerwear and personal accessories each have a different role. Neipclova’s approach starts with fit, texture, practical use and the feeling of being at ease.

Infancy & childhood

Discuss sizing, fabric feel, ease of changing, washing and the suitability of every material and component.

Pregnancy & everyday life

Consider changing fit, freedom of movement and the wearer’s own comfort preferences.

Later life & care settings

Prioritise practical dressing, laundering and the requirements of the intended care environment.

Korean culture, carried into daily wear.

Traditional silhouettes, thoughtful construction and the dignity of everyday dress inform our design direction. Contemporary use and the individual wearer remain at the centre.

A garment that belongs to you.

Neipclova encourages a conversation about one well-chosen garment: its purpose, fit, material and care. Personalisation and availability are discussed individually rather than assumed for every item.

For infant, maternity or clinical use, product suitability must be assessed specifically. General wellness clothing is not a substitute for prescribed medical garments or a care facility’s requirements.
