01 / YACHTS FIRST

A refined interior, built for its environment.

Yacht salons

A yacht salon is the main social interior. Material appearance must work with daylight, sea reflections, cabinetry and service access.

Decorative positions
Cabinet fronts, sideboards, bulkhead feature panels, window surrounds and interior door facings.
Detail priorities
Grain continuity around adjoining panels, edge returns, access to equipment and glare near windows.
Environment
Cabin humidity, sunlight, cleaning and vessel movement. State each exposure in the brief.
Review sample
Use a representative curved or joined panel with the actual base and bonding construction. Interior concepts are not deck or immersion qualifications.

Yacht cabins

Cabins combine sleeping, storage and compact circulation. Comfortable surfaces still need clear maintenance and ventilation details.

Decorative positions
Wardrobe faces, berth surrounds, headboard panels and internal partition facings.
Detail priorities
Curved geometry, opening clearances, replaceable access panels and edge protection in narrow passages.
Environment
Repeated contact, luggage impact and possible condensation near cooler surfaces.
Review sample
Check the proposed assembly, cleaning access and any vessel-specific fire requirements with the responsible project team.

Ship interior metal panels

The supplied technical material describes decorative PP sheets laminated to metal for ship interior panels. Specify the selected construction rather than an interchangeable film.

Layer logic
Decorative surface / selected bonding interface / metal backing / project-specific support assembly. This is functional logic, not a fixed manufacturing stack.
Joint design
Plan returns, cut edges, fastener locations, trim pieces and removable service access with the panel manufacturer.
Durability
Assess adhesion after relevant moisture and temperature exposure, corrosion protection at edges and the response of bonded layers to movement.
Documentation
Request the actual grade drawing, bonding process, tested substrate and applicable marine documentation. The entire installed assembly needs the appropriate review.

Moisture & cleaning

Durability is found at interfaces as much as on the visible face. A clean-looking panel can still fail at an exposed edge or incompatible joint.

Moisture path
Identify condensation, spray, wiping and any potential ingress at cut edges, seams, openings or fasteners.
Cleaning brief
List the real detergent or disinfectant, concentration, contact time and frequency. Follow the relevant product instructions and establish compatibility.
Evaluation
Examine colour, gloss, texture, swelling, adhesion and edge lift after the agreed exposure. Record the method and baseline photographs.
Maintenance
Agree accessible seams, replaceable panels and inspection points. No universal waterproof, salt-proof or solvent-proof claim is made.

03 / BEYOND THE YACHT

Different vehicles. Different requirements.

Automotive interiors

Decorative trim must fit the part geometry and the vehicle environment, with interfaces kept clear of safety systems.

Positions
Door inserts, console accents, dashboard decorative features and storage trim.
Geometry
Compound curves, edge returns, clip interfaces and clearances around controls.
Evaluation
Heat cycling, UV, rubbing, cleaners, odour / emissions and adhesion on the actual part.
Scope
No generic architectural film is presented as a qualified vehicle trim or exterior wrap. Safety-critical components require the vehicle team’s review.
Discuss this application ↗

Passenger trains

Rail interiors combine long service life with dense public use and practical replacement during maintenance.

Positions
Interior wall facings, partition surfaces, cabinet panels and non-critical table or trim features.
Geometry
Large repeat panels, vibration-prone joints, fastener positions and access panels.
Evaluation
Passenger wear, cleaning agents, impact, vibration-related interfaces and operator-required fire / smoke documentation.
Scope
The rail builder and operator define the grade, complete assembly and qualification process. A surface selection does not establish rail acceptance.
Discuss this application ↗

Aircraft interiors

Aviation decorative changes are engineering changes. Appearance is developed within the approved cabin-interior process.

Positions
Potential sidewall / partition decorative finishes, cabinetry faces and other approved interior panel positions.
Geometry
Weight, formed shapes, panel interfaces and maintenance access matter alongside the visual finish.
Evaluation
The aircraft team defines flammability and other applicable requirements for the actual alteration and construction.
Scope
Do not cover evacuation information or interfere with safety systems. These are development concepts, not aircraft-qualified Neipclova products.
